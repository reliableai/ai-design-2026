Completion checklist

Required artifacts

  • Architecture: diagram + component responsibilities + interfaces
  • Decision log: make-vs-buy notes, model/provider choices, tradeoffs
  • Golden dataset: examples, labels, coverage rationale, known gaps
  • Scorecard: 3–6 quality dimensions with clear rubrics
  • Evaluation harness: reproducible runs, versioned configs, CI-friendly
  • Error taxonomy: top failure modes with examples and frequency
  • Observability: logs/metrics/traces, dashboards, alert thresholds
  • Guardrails: runtime assertions, tool constraints, safe-mode behavior
  • Runbook: rollout/rollback, incident response, escalation path
  • Final report: results + uncertainty notes + what to ship vs not

Recommended add-ons

  • Prompt/model versioning policy (semver-like) and changelog
  • Cost budget monitoring + cache strategy (where relevant)
  • Human-in-the-loop or review workflows for high-risk actions
  • Red-teaming checklist for safety and prompt injection risks
  • Postmortem template and a “next 30 days” roadmap

“Good” looks like this

Evaluation

  • Your scorecard tracks multiple dimensions, not one number
  • Your golden dataset is documented: what it covers and what it misses
  • Runs are reproducible (same inputs ⇒ same outputs within expected variance)
  • Experiments include at least one ablation/comparison with a clear hypothesis

Operations

  • You can explain failure modes and show how the system fails safely
  • Logs are structured and traceable end-to-end
  • There is an explicit rollback and safe-mode plan
  • Cost/latency budgets are monitored and enforced (where feasible)
