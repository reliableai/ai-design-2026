# Learning objectives

Build effective, reliable, maintainable AI systems at scale. Solve complex problems by decomposing them in a principled way. Ship a reliable AI feature into an existing product.

Use AI to build AI. Leverage AI for development, evaluation, and system management. Understand what this unlocks and where the limits are.

Iterate towards success — rather than optimizing in the dark. Master the science of monitoring, analysis, and estimation. Stand up an evaluation harness you can run in CI.

Master the key conceptual abstractions. Build observability, cost controls, and incident-ready guardrails. Take lessons from software engineering past, but do so critically.

Separate signal from hype. Critically review emerging practices and proposed standards, learning what to use and where.
Think in systems. Understand which engineering principles carry forward and which new abstractions are essential. Know when not to use agents (and what to do instead).
