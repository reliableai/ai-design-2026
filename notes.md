# Learning objectives

Learn how to develop Effective, Reliable, Maintainable AI systems and how to do so at scale, solving complex problems by decomposing them in a principled, maangeable way

Use AI to build AI. Leverage AI for development, evaluation, and system management. Understand what this unlocks and where the limits are.                       
Iterate towards success - rather than optimizing in the dark. Master the science of monitoring, analysis, and estimation: measure business quality, detect failures, and quantify uncertainty before your users do. 

Understand the key conceptual abstractions and middleware we need to brave this new world. Take lessons from the software engineering past (the old pre-2026 world), but do so critically. Understand what are the key concepts we need, and how to conquer them, use them successfully to guide our development. 


In the process, we will review critically the emerging practices and proposed standards learning what and how to use where.


Think in systems. Understand which engineering principles carry forward and which new abstractions are essential for the AI-native era.
